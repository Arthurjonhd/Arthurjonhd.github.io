How it works

Three steps, no surprises

  1. Tell us the problem

    Call, email, or send the form. Describe it however you can — screenshots and "it just stopped working" are both fine.

  2. We diagnose and quote

    We find the actual cause and tell you what it takes to fix it, what it costs, and how long it will take. No work starts before you approve it.

  3. We fix it, on-site or remote

    Most issues are solved in one visit or one remote session. You get a plain-language summary of what was done.
